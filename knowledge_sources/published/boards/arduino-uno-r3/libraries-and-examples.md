# Enter examples through canonical recipes

For canonical board `arduino-uno-r3`, begin with recipe `uno-blink-built-in` when its stated outcome matches the learner's goal. Load the recipe before selecting source files, libraries, or tools, and preserve its verification fields. Previous compilation evidence helps choose a path but does not establish that the current device was uploaded, rebooted, observed through serial, or physically confirmed.
