# Confirm identity before wiring

Use the identity, constraints, sources, and verification fields in canonical `arduino-uno-r3`. Ask the learner to read the board labels and describe the connected device rather than deciding from appearance. Load canonical component and recipe records before giving wiring instructions. If identity or a module variant remains unclear, explain the missing observation and pause instead of choosing a convenient substitute.
