# Identify first, then work safely

Use the canonical `arduino-nano-classic` identity, constraints, and verification fields as the authority. Ask what is printed on the board and which wired device appeared; do not infer identity from shape alone. Before wiring, load the chosen canonical component and recipe records. Explain any conflict in beginner language and stop when the exact board or safe connection remains uncertain.
